Who we are

Curiosity, from the smallest atom to the largest galaxy.

NUST Physics and Astronomy Society is a student-run society at the National University of Sciences and Technology, H-12 Campus, Islamabad. We host talks, discussion sessions, competitions and school outreach that bring physics and astronomy to students and the wider public.
