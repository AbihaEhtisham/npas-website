Overview

A week for anyone curious about space

Space Week is NPAS's flagship event. Each year the society brings students, faculty and the public together for talks, competitions, outreach and a night under the stars.

This edition runs from 7 to 16 October 2026 on the NUST H-12 campus and in partner schools across Islamabad. Everyone is welcome, and no prior knowledge is needed.

What to expect

Four ways to take part

Research talks

Faculty and students present current work in space science.

Astronomy Night

Telescopes, stargazing and expert guidance on the SCME Ground.

Competitions

Online and on-campus contests with certificates and cash prizes.

School outreach

Volunteers bring space science to schools across Islamabad.
