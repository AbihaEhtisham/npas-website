About NPAS

A student-run society that explores the universe, from the smallest atoms to the largest cosmic bodies.

Our mission

To enlighten the masses about physics and astronomy

NUST Physics and Astronomy Society aims to enlighten the masses about different topics of physics and astronomy and to help people explore the intricacies of the universe.

From the smallest atoms to the largest cosmic bodies, we investigate everything that triggers the inquisitive nature of humans. For this purpose, we regularly conduct events and sessions related to physics and astronomy.

What we do

Four ways we bring people to the stars

Talks and sessions

Physics talks, discussion sessions and interviews with leading scientists.

Astronomy nights

Public stargazing with telescopes, held with the PakAstronomers Society.

Competitions

Astrophotography, art, writing and trivia for students across Pakistan.

School outreach

Volunteers visit schools to share the excitement of space and physics.
